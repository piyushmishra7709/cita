Who we are

Citabharat EV Pvt Ltd

Citabharat EV Pvt Ltd is based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, and sells under the brand Citabharat EV. It is the Indian company of CITA EV Limited (UK), a charger brand trusted in 70+ countries.

India’s charging network is growing fast, and operators need equipment, software and business guidance they can rely on. We connect CITA’s global engineering with Indian sites, utilities and customers.
