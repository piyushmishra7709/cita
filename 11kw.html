Frequently asked questions

Who Is the CITA Smart 11 kW EV Charger Ideal For?

The CITA Smart 11 kW EV charger is well suited for villas, premium apartments, and offices in India with a three-phase power supply. It is perfect for users who want faster charging than a standard 7 kW home unit without shifting to DC fast charging. This balance of speed and practicality makes it one of the best home EV chargers in India. Developed by the best EV charger manufacturer in India, it delivers efficient performance for both residential and workplace environments.

How Fast Can an EV Be Charged with an 11 kW Charger?

Charging time depends on battery capacity and current charge level. On average, most electric vehicles in India can be fully charged within 4 to 6 hours using an 11 kW EV charger. This is noticeably faster than 7 kW charging, making it ideal for daily home use and office parking. For drivers seeking the best electric car charger solutions in India, the 11 kW model offers the right combination of speed and efficiency.

Can the 11 kW EV Charger Be Installed Indoors or Outdoors?

Yes, the CITA Smart 11 kW EV charger is designed for both indoor and outdoor installations where three-phase power is available. It supports faster charging at home and efficient vehicle turnaround at offices. With IP65 and IK10 protection, it performs reliably in India’s heat and dust conditions. This versatility reinforces CITA’s reputation as a top EV charger company in India delivering durable charging solutions.

Is the 11 kW Charger Suitable for Both Homes and Workplaces?

Absolutely. The CITA Smart 11 kW EV charger is built to support residential garages as well as commercial office parking areas. Its faster charging capability ensures convenience for homeowners and improved efficiency for businesses. Recognised as one of the best EV chargers in India for three-phase setups, it provides stable, high-performance charging across different environments.

Does the 11 kW EV Charger Offer Smart App Control and Scheduling?

Yes. The CITA Smart 11 kW EV charger supports smart control through the CITA EV App. Users can monitor charging status, schedule sessions, and manage energy usage directly from their smartphones. These intelligent features enhance safety, control, and convenience for daily users. This smart ecosystem is why CITA EV is known for delivering some of the best electric car charger solutions in India.
