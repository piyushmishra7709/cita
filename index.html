FAQ

Questions we are asked most

Which chargers does CITA offer?

AC chargers from 7 kW to 44 kW, the Dual ECO Pillar, and DC chargers from 30 kW to 480 kW.

What is the FOCO model?

Franchise Owned, Company Operated. You provide the site and capital, CITA operates the station and shares the revenue with you.

Do CITA chargers work with Indian EVs?

Yes. They are built for popular Indian EVs such as the Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV, BYD Atto 3, Hyundai Kona and Mahindra XUV400.

Is installation support included?

Authorised installation partners handle site work. CITA provides commissioning, software setup, updates and troubleshooting.
