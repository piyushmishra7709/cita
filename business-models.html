01
Own the station

Charge Point Operator (CPO)

Own and run public charging stations and earn from every kWh sold. You invest in the site and keep the revenue.

How it works

  1. Choose site and sizeWe confirm location, power availability and charger mix.
  2. Install and commissionChargers, cabling, canopy and software go live.
  3. List and operateYour stations appear in the CITA EV App and others.
  4. Earn and scaleAdd chargers as utilisation grows.

What is included

  • Chargers and installation
  • CPMS dashboard and mobile app
  • Staff training and launch support
  • AMC and remote monitoring

Revenue and benefits

  • Charging fees per kWh
  • Idle and parking fees
  • Advertising and café tie-ups

02
Software only

CPMS software

Already have chargers? Manage them on the CITA OCPP platform. Monthly subscription per charger.

How it works

  1. Connect chargersAny OCPP charger links to the CITA platform.
  2. Set tariffsPricing, users, groups and access rules.
  3. Go liveDrivers use the app, RFID or QR to start sessions.
  4. Report and improveTrack uptime, energy and revenue.

What is included

  • CPMS dashboard
  • White-label app option
  • Billing and GST invoices
  • Support and updates

Revenue and benefits

  • Keep all revenue from your chargers
  • Add white-label B2B or B2C app income
  • Run fleet and corporate accounts

Quick facts

03
Earn without operating

FOCO: Franchise Owned, Company Operated

You bring the site and capital. CITA runs the station for you. Hands-off ownership with a revenue share.

How it works

  1. AgreementRevenue-share terms and site commitment are signed.
  2. BuildCITA designs and installs the station.
  3. CITA operatesBilling, maintenance, uptime and helpline are handled.
  4. Monthly payoutYou receive your share with a clear statement.

What is included

  • Full station build
  • Branding and signage
  • Operations and maintenance
  • Customer helpline

Revenue and benefits

  • Agreed share of station revenue
  • Monthly payout
  • Site value uplift from EV traffic

Quick facts

InvestmentMedium to high
Ideal forOwners who want income without day-to-day work
